## Start with what you want to do

- Experimentation: software access, support and required skills matter.
- Demonstrations: clarify control, transport and supervision.
- Work or chores: a video does not prove the product can do the task out of the box in your environment.

### [R1, G1 or Booster K1?](/en/models/)

These are not three interchangeable household assistants. Start with the task, not the height.
