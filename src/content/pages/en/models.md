## In brief: which should you investigate?

**Our editorial guidance**, not a performance ranking:

| If your goal is… | Investigate | What to clarify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An enthusiast humanoid for supervised demonstrations | R1, starting with the base configuration | Ask to see exactly what ships; do not buy on the promise of a future update. |
| Programming a compact education or research platform | K1 Geek / Education; request an R1 EDU quote as an alternative | Check SDK access, runnable examples, support and battery life for the selected version. |
| Experimenting with grasping and manipulation | G1 EDU with hands specified in the quotation | Hands are optional in the official matrix: not a capability included in the base G1 price.[2](#sources) |
| Automating a repetitive task in a small business | A task demonstration before choosing hardware | The sources consulted do not establish that any of these packages performs your job autonomously. |
| A household assistant ready to tidy up or do chores | Do not order based on this comparison | We have not verified a ready-to-use solution for these tasks. |

## Differences that affect the purchase

Manufacturer-declared specifications, not our measurements. Degrees of freedom describe joints, not intelligence or usefulness.

| Factor | Unitree R1 | Unitree G1 | Booster K1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Size and weight | About **123 cm / 29 kg**.[1](#sources) | **132 cm**; about **35 kg** base, **35 kg+** EDU.[2](#sources) | About **95 cm / 19.5 kg**.[3](#sources) |
| Declared articulation | **20–26** degrees of freedom in the family description; do not assign the maximum to the base model.[1](#sources) | **23** base; **23–43** EDU depending on options.[2](#sources) | **22**, across the three versions.[3](#sources) |
| Advanced development | The store reserves it for **EDU**.[1](#sources) | Base without secondary development; **EDU** supported.[2](#sources)<span>, </span>[5](#sources) | Declared supported on **Geek, Education and Professional**.[3](#sources) |
| Hands and grasping | Specify equipment and capabilities in the quote; the starting price alone does not establish them.[1](#sources) | Optional three-finger hands on EDU; not listed on the base model.[2](#sources) | The sheet lists four degrees of freedom per arm, not grasping hands equivalent to a configured G1 EDU.[3](#sources) |
| Battery life | No figure reported here: not verified in the store sheet consulted.[1](#sources) | About **2 hours** declared, without an operating cycle comparable to the K1's.[2](#sources) | Geek **30 minutes / 2 Ah**; Education and Professional **80 minutes / 5 Ah**, walking at **0.4 m/s**, according to Booster's sheet consulted on 12 November 2026.[3](#sources) |

### R1: a low starting price, but the version matters

The store combines promotional wording about open interfaces with the explicit warning “Only the EDU version supports secondary development”. For a software project, we treat that distinction as decisive: request an EDU quote, included access rights and a demonstration of your program. The starting price does not establish development access on the base version.[1](#sources)

### G1: the hand configuration matters more than the video

The matrix distinguishes base and EDU, optional hands and additional computing. Unitree also warns that some demonstrated functions are still under development/testing and advises private buyers to understand humanoid limitations before purchasing.[2](#sources) For us, this is sufficient reason to request a demonstration **on the hardware actually offered**, not a generic G1 family video.

### K1: Geek is not simply a discounted Education

Booster declares development support for all versions, but distinguishes batteries, computing and advertised warranty coverage. Geek has a 2 Ah battery and a stated three-month warranty; Education and Professional have 5 Ah batteries and a stated one-year warranty.[3](#sources) These are the manufacturer's advertised coverage periods, not an assessment of a European buyer's statutory rights.

**Battery life to confirm:** Booster's sheet consulted on 12 November 2026 lists 30 minutes for Geek and 80 for Education/Professional at 0.4 m/s (different conditions from the 1.1 m/s reported on 31/10). Test conditions affect the result: ask for written confirmation for the selected variant and task.[3](#sources) We do not treat TOPS figures from different computing platforms as a common benchmark.

## Skills and a demonstration before ordering

For existing features, request operator training, space requirements, stopping procedures and an exact list of functions active on delivery. For development, our checklist is more demanding: someone responsible for the software, version-specific documentation, a buildable/runnable example on the robot, simulation and recovery after an error. We have not personally verified the SDKs or ease of installation.

For a small business, define an acceptance test: **which object, which environment, how many repetitions, what human intervention and what constitutes failure**. Distinguish a teleoperated demonstration from a programmed sequence and an autonomous decision. Without this comparison, we do not estimate staffing savings or return on investment.

These are heavy motorised platforms, not toys to leave among people or animals. Unitree explicitly requires a safe distance for G1.[2](#sources) Ask a competent person to demonstrate safety measures and recovery procedures in line with the manufacturer's instructions. Do not deliberately cause falls.

## Independent evidence to update

In the sample consulted on 23 October, R1/G1/K1 material was dominated by announcements, spec sheets and development or simulation discussions; we had not found hands-on owner reviews comparable to those available for the Go2. Before deciding, look for current independent evidence and check its context.

## What to request in a European quotation

- Exact version code; included hands, computing, batteries, charger, controller and accessories.
- Software access, licences, cloud dependencies and costs after any free periods.
- Destination-specific total: shipping, insurance, taxes, any duties and responsibility for importing. Do not assume every seller ships from within the EU.
- Written delivery date, applicable model documentation and instructions the operator can understand.
- Support contact, repair location, spare parts, return freight costs for faults and terms for private or business use.
- Demonstration of the agreed task and contractual remedies if the delivered configuration differs.

**Our criterion:** choose the package that demonstrates your use case and makes costs and support verifiable, not the best video. Continue with the [buying guide](/en/buy/) or read [how we assess sources](/en/method/).
