> Some buyers look for a used quadruped because new costs too much — and ask where to buy one, without finding an established market.[1] Used can be good value, but protection and risks differ from new: this guide orders them before you pay.

## Who you buy from changes your protection

| Seller | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Another private individual | The EU consumer protection described here does not cover private-to-private sales: everything rests on your contract. Test live and pay with a traceable method. |
| Professional trader (even for used goods) | Used goods from a trader remain covered by the legal guarantee, with the possibility in some countries to agree a reduced period, never below one year. Get it in writing.[2] |

We found no established second-hand market for these robots: one buyer looked for a reference point in the US without receiving consolidated guidance.[1] Distrust sellers in a hurry and prices without a stated configuration.

## The checklist before paying

- **Live test**: power-on, walking, fall and recovery, working cameras and LiDAR. A video is not enough.
- **Battery**: charge cycles, remaining runtime, swelling; ask the price of a new battery as a reference.
- **Software**: firmware version, included access, any locks tied to the previous account; confirm advanced mode can be activated (new users report activation problems even on new units).[3]
- **Damage**: scuffed knees and legs are normal (hands-on tests document them); cracked shells and noisy motors are not.[4]
- **Paperwork**: original proof of purchase, reason for selling, remaining warranty and whether it transfers.

**Our criterion:** a live-tested used unit with a healthy battery beats an unseen new one at a suspicious price. If the seller refuses the test, stop. Continue with the [buying guide](/en/buy/) or the [Go2 comparison](/en/go2/).
