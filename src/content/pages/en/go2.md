> **Key Takeaways:** for a hobby or app-controlled use, compare Air and Pro against the features you want. For a software project, confirm EDU access and equipment. Unitree's matrix indicates partial development support for X: clarify its scope before treating it as an EDU alternative.[1](#sources)

A Unitree Go2 may interest you as a hobby or for a business task: those are different buying decisions. This guide compares documentation and attributed third-party tests; we have not tested the hardware. For a business, the decisive check is a demonstration of the required task on the proposed configuration, with development work and any operator disclosed.

## More than a speed difference

Air, Pro, X and EDU differ in features, accessories and development access. This table reports Unitree's matrix checked on 30 October 2026, not our test results.[1](#sources)

| Feature | Air | Pro | X | EDU |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Graphical programming | Listed | Listed | Listed | Listed |
| ISS 2.0 person following | Not listed as included | Listed | Listed | Listed |
| Official advanced development | Not listed | Not listed | Partial support; clarify scope | Listed; confirm configuration |
| Stated runtime | 1–2 hours | 1–2 hours | 1–2 hours | 2–4 hours for the listed configuration |

## Two meanings of programming

The graphical programming listed in the table is not full access to motors, sensors and algorithms. Unitree refers secondary-development questions to its manual: the EDU tick or partial X symbol does not describe every available interface.[1](#sources)

For development, ask which SDKs (software development kits), APIs (programming interfaces) and control levels are accessible in the quoted configuration. Confirm runnable examples, documentation and included support. Do not base the purchase on an assumption that an unofficial integration is equivalent to manufacturer support.

## What payload and runtime mean

Stated runtime is not guaranteed working time in your environment. A payload on the robot's back does not prove it can load or unload objects itself; charging dock compatibility does not mean the dock is included. Before assessing it for a business task, check the operating cycle, supervision and actual equipment.[1](#sources)

## Compare like-for-like prices

On 30 October 2026, the international matrix lists Air at USD 1,600, Pro at USD 2,800 and X at USD 4,500, excluding taxes and freight; EDU requires a quote.[1](#sources) The store separates controller options and makes the buyer responsible for applicable duties, taxes and customs clearance.[2](#sources)

As a reference for Italy, on 31 October 2026 Abra lists Go2 Air **from EUR 2,576 excluding VAT**, with shipping and customs duty in Italy included according to the seller. The list requires confirmation in an updated quotation: check equipment, VAT, support and the final cost for your destination. Recheck on 12 November 2026: the versions/pricing page is still live from EUR 2,576, but the single-Air sheet points to an updated quote and presents the AS2 as the replacement platform.[3](#sources)

Compare a European quote for the same version, battery, accessories and support. Converting the headline price is not enough: use the [Italy and EU buying guide](/en/buy/) to prepare questions about delivered cost and seller terms.

## A discrepancy to resolve

Abra's sales page describes person following on Go2 Air, whereas Unitree does not list ISS 2.0 as included on Air.[1](#sources)[3](#sources) These descriptions do not establish whether they refer to the same feature or additional equipment. Abra's list includes an optional UWB module for the Follow function: one possible explanation is that following on Air requires that accessory, but this still needs a demonstration.[3](#sources) If following is the reason for buying, request a demonstration on the exact version and package.

## What third-party tests add

In the review updated on 14 September 2026, FauxHammer values the Go2 Pro mainly for fun and robotics curiosity, rather than everyday household help. This is the reviewer's assessment of the Pro, not proof that no configuration can serve a practical task.[4](#sources)

New Atlas describes learning the app and controls, and documents startup difficulties and wear on rough surfaces during its Pro test. These are matters to explore in the manual and a guided demonstration, not instructions to provoke falls or stress-test a robot before buying.[5](#sources)

Two r/unitree threads describe a LiDAR issue and problems activating advanced mode. These are individual historical accounts, not our diagnoses or reliability statistics. They do not establish that the issue affects current firmware or the unit you will buy.[6](#sources)[7](#sources)

## Get these confirmed before paying

- Exact version, battery, controller and accessories included.
- The required feature demonstrated on that configuration, with any operator disclosed.
- Official software access and development work required.
- Delivered cost for your country and written support conditions.

If these answers are missing, postpone the version choice. For a second-hand purchase, also use the [used robot checklist](/en/used/); to see how we distinguish statements from tests, read the [editorial method](/en/method/). Neither route replaces checking the actual configuration offered.
