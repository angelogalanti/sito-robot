## Same product, same offer?

Specify version, accessories, battery and included software. A base platform cannot be compared directly with a configured package that includes support.

## Delivered cost and availability

Request a written total for your destination: VAT, shipping, any duties and customs clearance. “In stock” needs confirmation of shipping origin and delivery. A preorder is not immediate availability.

## Faults, support and returns

Identify the seller and where the robot must be sent if it fails. Ask what support covers, who pays shipping and what conditions apply. Published terms do not replace checking the rights applicable to your purchase.

## Language is not a country

An Italian page does not establish that the seller is based in Italy. An English page does not guarantee availability across Europe. Confirm destination and service location.

## Get these confirmed before paying

- Exact version, battery, controller and accessories included.
- The required feature demonstrated on that configuration, with any operator disclosed.
- Official software access and development work required.
- Delivered cost for your country and written support conditions.
