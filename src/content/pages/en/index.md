## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What does a robot dog really cost?</summary>
The price on the manufacturer's site is only the start: shipping, customs and accessories change the total. The [Go2 comparison](/en/go2/) explains versions and costs, the [buying-from-Europe guide](/en/buy/) what to check before ordering.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Base model or developer version?</summary>
It depends on whether you want to use it or program it: those are two different purchases. The [R1, G1 and K1 comparison](/en/models/) starts from that choice.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use it at school or in a lab?</summary>
Yes, but you need space, supervision and a quote that includes everything. The [school and research guide](/en/edu/) lists what to check.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is used worth it?</summary>
Only if you verify the seller, the configuration and the battery condition before looking at the price. The [used guide](/en/used/) explains how.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why no scores or winners?</summary>
Because we compare documents, we do not test the hardware: we give you verified facts and leave the choice to you. The [method](/en/method/) explains how we work.
</details>
