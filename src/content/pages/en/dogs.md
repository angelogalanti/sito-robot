Robot dogs are the most mature category: they walk, sell and work today, but features and prices vary widely between versions. Start from your use case, then compare the version, delivery costs and used options.

## Start with what you want to do

- Hobby: compare included features and space requirements without expecting household help.
- Programming: confirm the access required by your project, not just the presence of sensors.
- Business use: define a task and ask for a demonstration on that configuration.
